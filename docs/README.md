# README: OctoAcme Project Management Processes Summary and Links

The OctoAcme Project Management Docs describe a lightweight, collaborative approach for delivering product features, services, and integrations. This page is the starting point for understanding the lifecycle, responsibilities, and working practices covered by the process documents.

## Process lifecycle

OctoAcme begins with **initiation**: define the problem, goal, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, and initial risks in a Project One-pager, then confirm alignment and team availability before moving to **planning**. Planning turns the approved initiative into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, dependencies, QA approach, milestones, and a release plan.

During **execution**, the team delivers small increments through the project board and PR workflow, with testing, review, demos, and progress tracking. The PM and Product Lead coordinate delivery and priorities; Developers implement and test, QA/Testing validates acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. **Release** requires acceptance criteria and CI/security checks to pass, release notes and rollback plans to be ready, and post-deploy verification and stakeholder communication.

After each sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team holds a **retrospective** and tracks a few actionable improvements in the backlog. **Risk management** runs throughout planning and execution: owners assess, mitigate, and review risks and dependencies in the risk register, escalating blockers through the PM, Product Lead, and sponsor as needed. **Communication** follows a regular cadence of team standups, weekly delivery/PM syncs, milestone or monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalation, using project documentation as the source of truth.
